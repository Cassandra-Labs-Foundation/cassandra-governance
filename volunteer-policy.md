# Volunteer Policy

## Cassandra Labs Contributor Agreement

_Modeled after Apache Software Foundation and Linux Foundation contributor agreements_

***

### Why This Agreement Exists

Cassandra Labs builds public-goods infrastructure and collective intelligence tools. Like open-source software projects, we rely on contributions from a community of collaborators. This Agreement clarifies the terms under which you contribute, protects both you and the project, and ensures we can freely use and distribute the work.

**This is not an employment agreement.** You are not an employee, contractor, or agent of Cassandra Labs. You will not receive compensation for your contributions. You contribute because you believe in the mission and want to be part of building something meaningful.

#### 1. Definitions

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship—including software, documentation, designs, research, written content, data, or other materials—that you intentionally submit to Cassandra Labs for inclusion in any Cassandra project. "Submit" means any form of communication sent to Cassandra (pull request, email, message, shared document, etc.), excluding communications conspicuously marked "Not a Contribution."

**"Project"** means any initiative, repository, product, or body of work managed by Cassandra Labs.

***

#### 2. Grant of Rights

**2.1 Copyright License.** You grant to Cassandra Labs and to all recipients of works distributed by Cassandra a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable license to:

* Reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, and distribute your Contributions
* Sublicense any or all of the foregoing rights to third parties

**2.2 Patent License.** You grant to Cassandra Labs and to all recipients of works distributed by Cassandra a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable patent license to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale, import, and otherwise transfer your Contributions—where such license applies only to patent claims licensable by you that are necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone or in combination with the Project.

**2.3 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by law, you waive any moral rights in your Contributions and agree not to assert them.

**2.4 Right to Grant.** You represent that you are legally entitled to grant the above licenses. If your employer or institution has rights to intellectual property you create, you represent that you have received permission to make Contributions, or that your employer has waived such rights.

***

#### 3. Your Representations

You represent that:

* Each Contribution is your original creation, or you have sufficient rights to submit it under these terms
* Your Contributions do not violate any third party's intellectual property or other rights
* You are not aware of any claims, liens, or encumbrances on any Contribution
* You will promptly notify Cassandra if you become aware of any facts that would make these representations inaccurate

***

#### 4. No Compensation or Equity

**4.1 Volunteer Contribution.** You understand and agree that:

* Your Contributions are made voluntarily and without expectation of payment
* You will not receive wages, salary, fees, equity, profit-sharing, or any other form of compensation
* Cassandra has no obligation to use, include, or maintain your Contributions
* Cassandra has no obligation to credit or attribute your Contributions (though we generally do)

**4.2 No Equity Rights.** You understand that this Agreement grants you no ownership interest, equity stake, or financial interest in Cassandra Labs or any project, product, entity, or venture that may derive from Cassandra's work—whether such entity exists now or is formed in the future.

**4.3 Future Opportunities.** If Cassandra Labs or any affiliated entity forms a company, launches a commercial venture, or creates employment opportunities related to work you contributed to, you may be invited to participate—but you have no right or entitlement to such participation. Any such arrangement would require a separate agreement with its own terms.

***

#### 5. Project Governance

**5.1 Cassandra's Rights.** Cassandra Labs retains sole discretion over:

* Whether to accept or reject any Contribution
* How to use, modify, license, or distribute Contributions
* Project direction, priorities, and governance
* Who may participate in Projects and in what capacity

**5.2 Open Licensing.** Cassandra generally releases its public-goods work under open-source or Creative Commons licenses. Contributions to such projects will be distributed under the applicable project license.

**5.3 Commercial Use.** You acknowledge that Cassandra or its affiliates may use Contributions in commercial products or services. You have no claim to revenue, royalties, or proceeds from any such use.

***

#### 6. Confidentiality

You agree not to disclose non-public information about Cassandra's projects, strategies, partnerships, or internal operations without authorization. This does not restrict you from discussing your own Contributions or publicly available project information.

***

#### 7. Term and Withdrawal

**7.1 Effective Period.** This Agreement applies to all Contributions you submit after the Effective Date.

**7.2 Withdrawal.** You may stop contributing at any time, for any reason, without notice. However, the licenses you have granted for Contributions already submitted remain in effect permanently.

**7.3 Removal.** Cassandra may revoke your access to project resources, communication channels, or contribution systems at any time, for any reason.

***

#### 8. General Provisions

**8.1 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the complete agreement between you and Cassandra regarding Contributions and supersedes any prior understandings.

**8.2 Amendments.** Cassandra may update this Agreement. Continued contributions after notice of changes constitutes acceptance. Changes do not apply retroactively to Contributions already submitted.

**8.3 Severability.** If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

**8.4 No Agency.** Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship.

**8.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
