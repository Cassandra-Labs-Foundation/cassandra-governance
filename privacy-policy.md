# Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy

**Cassandra Labs Foundation, Inc.** A 501(c)(3) Charitable, Educational, and Scientific Organization

***

#### 1. Introduction

Cassandra Labs Foundation ("Cassandra Labs," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our members and website visitors. This Privacy Policy describes what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

This policy applies to all data Cassandra Labs collects or receives through:

* The Cassandra Labs membership portal
* The [cassandralabs.org](https://cassandralabs.org/) website
* Email communications
* Data received from third parties under data-sharing agreements (including OpenClaims usage data received from Cheda, Inc.)

For details on how your data is used specifically for research purposes, see our [Research Participation Consent](https://cassandra-labs.gitbook.io/openclaim/research-participation-consent).

***

#### 2. What Data We Collect

**2a. Data You Provide Directly**

When you join the Cassandra Labs Association or use the membership portal, you may provide:

* **Identity information**: Name, email address, phone number (optional), and physical address
* **Account credentials**: Email and password used to log into the membership portal (authentication managed by Supabase)
* **Membership preferences**: Participation interests, meeting preferences, and initiative interests
* **Communications**: Any messages you send to us via email

**2b. Data Collected Automatically**

When you visit [cassandralabs.org](https://cassandralabs.org/) or use the membership portal, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically collects basic server logs, which may include:

* IP address
* Browser type and version
* Pages visited and timestamps

We do not use analytics tools, tracking cookies, or advertising pixels on our website.

**2c. Data from Third Parties**

If you join the Cassandra Labs Association through OpenClaims and consent to research participation, Cheda, Inc. ("Cheda") may share anonymized usage data with us under a data-sharing agreement. This is described in detail in our [Research Participation Consent](https://cassandra-labs.gitbook.io/openclaim/research-participation-consent) and only applies to members who have affirmatively opted in. Cheda's own handling of your data is governed by [Cheda's Privacy Policy](https://cheda.com/privacy).

We may establish data-sharing agreements with additional partners in the future. If we do, they will be identified in this section and you will be notified by email of any material changes, with the opportunity to withdraw your consent before the changes take effect.

**2d. Payment Data**

Membership dues are processed through Stripe. Cassandra Labs does not store your full payment card number. Stripe may retain payment metadata (last four digits, billing address, transaction records) under its own privacy policy. See [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

***

#### 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

* **Membership administration**: Managing your account, processing dues, tracking participation, and communicating with you about Association activities
* **Communications**: Sending meeting invitations, announcements, and other Association correspondence via Proton Mail
* **Research**: If you have consented to research participation, using your anonymized and aggregated data for research in furtherance of our 501(c)(3) mission (see [Research Participation Consent](https://cassandra-labs.gitbook.io/openclaim/research-participation-consent))
* **Legal compliance**: Meeting our obligations under applicable law

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing to third parties, or profiling.

***

#### 4. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell, rent, license, or trade your personal data — whether identifiable or anonymized — to third parties for commercial purposes. We share data only with the following categories of service providers, who process data on our behalf:

| Provider                                              | Purpose                             | Data Shared                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| [**Supabase**](https://supabase.com/privacy)          | Database hosting and authentication | Account data, member records                               |
| [**Stripe**](https://stripe.com/privacy)              | Payment processing                  | Payment and billing data                                   |
| [**Proton**](https://proton.me/legal/privacy)         | Email communications                | Email address, message content                             |
| [**Vercel**](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) | Website and portal hosting          | Server logs (IP, browser, timestamps)                      |
| [**Cheda, Inc.**](https://cheda.com/privacy)          | Research data source (inbound only) | Anonymized OpenClaims usage data (consenting members only) |

We may also disclose data if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

***

#### 5. Data Retention

We retain your membership data for as long as your membership is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to fulfill legal, accounting, or administrative obligations. We do not guarantee perpetual retention of any data and may delete records in the ordinary course of operations.

If you request deletion of your data (see Section 6), we will remove your identifiable data within thirty (30) days, except where retention is required by law or where data has already been anonymized for research purposes.

***

#### 6. Your Rights

All members have the right to:

* **Access**: Request a copy of the identifiable personal data we hold about you (data that has been anonymized for research is no longer linked to you and cannot be retrieved)
* **Correction**: Request that we correct inaccurate data
* **Deletion**: Request that we delete your identifiable data, subject to any legal obligations requiring retention and except where data has already been anonymized for research purposes
* **Withdrawal of research consent**: Withdraw your research participation at any time (see [Research Participation Consent, Section 6](https://cassandra-labs.gitbook.io/openclaim/research-participation-consent))
* **Inquire**: Ask questions about how your data is used

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 10. We will acknowledge your request within seven (7) days and complete it within thirty (30) days, or notify you if additional time is needed.

***

#### 7. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

* **Right to know**: You may request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources of that information, and our purposes for collecting it.
* **Right to delete**: You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
* **Right to non-discrimination**: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.
* **No sale of personal information**: Cassandra Labs does not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at the address in Section 10. We will verify your identity before processing your request and respond within forty-five (45) days.

***

#### 8. International Members

Cassandra Labs is based in the United States. If you are located outside the United States — including in the European Union or Nigeria — your personal data is transferred to and processed in the United States, where our service providers (Supabase, Stripe, Vercel) maintain their infrastructure. These transfers are supported by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) maintained by our service providers in their terms of service, as well as your explicit consent to join a US-based organization.

**European Union Residents (GDPR)**

If you are located in the European Economic Area, our legal basis for processing your data is:

* **Consent**: For research participation (which you may withdraw at any time)
* **Contractual necessity**: For membership administration and providing services you have requested
* **Legitimate interests**: For maintaining the security of our systems and fulfilling our organizational mission

You have the additional right to:

* **Data portability**: Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format
* **Restrict processing**: Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
* **Lodge a complaint**: File a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority

**Other International Jurisdictions**

If you are located in a jurisdiction with data protection laws that provide rights beyond those listed in Section 6, we will make reasonable efforts to honor those rights upon request. Contact us at the address in Section 10.

***

#### 9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These include:

* Database access restricted to authorized personnel (currently the Foundation President)
* Authentication managed by Supabase with industry-standard security practices
* Email communications sent via Proton Mail's end-to-end encrypted infrastructure
* Payment processing handled by Stripe's PCI-compliant systems

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to maintaining safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we hold.

***

#### 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. For material changes to what data we collect, how we use it, or your rights, we will notify members by email before the changes take effect.

***

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your rights:
